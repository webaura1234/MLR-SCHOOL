## How This Strategy Works

Each cluster has:
- **A Pillar Page** — a comprehensive, evergreen guide (1,500–3,000 words) targeting a broad keyword
- **Supporting Articles** — focused posts (800–1,500 words) targeting specific long-tail keywords
- **Internal Linking** — all supporting articles link back to the pillar page; the pillar links forward to supporting articles
- **CTA on every post** — every article points to `/admission`

## Internal Linking Strategy

### Hub-and-Spoke Model
Each cluster has one pillar page that receives links from all supporting articles. Supporting articles cross-link to related pillars in adjacent clusters.

### Mandatory Links on Every Blog Post
1. Link to `/admission` via the CTA at the end of every article
2. Link to the cluster's pillar page within the article body
3. Link to 2–3 related posts via the "Related Articles" section

### Key Pages That Must Receive Links
